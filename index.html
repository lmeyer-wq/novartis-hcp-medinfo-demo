Immunexis — Dosing & Administration

Medical Information · reviewed content · Updated Jun 2026 · 3 min read
Immunexis is a fictional product used for demonstration only.

Immunexis is a fully human monoclonal antibody that selectively inhibits interleukin-23 (IL-23) by binding its p19 subunit. It is indicated for adults with moderate-to-severe plaque psoriasis and active psoriatic arthritis.

Recommended dosing

  • 150 mg by subcutaneous injection at Week 0 and Week 4, then every 12 weeks thereafter.
  • The same schedule applies for plaque psoriasis and psoriatic arthritis.
  • If a dose is missed, administer as soon as possible, then resume the regular 12-week schedule.

Administration & storage

  • Supplied as a 150 mg/mL single-dose prefilled pen for subcutaneous use.
  • Store refrigerated at 2–8 °C in the original carton to protect from light; do not freeze or shake.
  • May be kept at room temperature (up to 25 °C) once, for up to 24 hours.
Safety note. Evaluate patients for tuberculosis before initiation and avoid starting during an active, serious infection. If you observe a suspected adverse reaction, please report it so it reaches Pharmacovigilance.
